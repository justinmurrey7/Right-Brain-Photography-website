About Me

Hey, I’m Justin.

Photography isn’t just what I do — it’s how I tell stories. I’m the photographer behind Right Brain Photography, and I focus on capturing real moments the way they actually feel.

Whether it’s a family laughing between poses, an athlete locked into the game, or a quiet moment at an event — those are the moments that matter most.

Why I Picked Up a Camera

I started photography because I didn’t want moments to disappear. Life moves fast — kids grow up, seasons change, and events come and go — and photos are one of the few ways we get to slow time down.

What began as a creative outlet quickly turned into a passion for preserving memories that feel authentic and meaningful.

My Style & What You Can Expect

My approach is relaxed and people-first. I’ll guide you when you need direction and step back when moments unfold naturally.

  • A laid-back, friendly experience
  • Professional quality with a personal touch
  • Photos that feel real — not stiff or over-posed
