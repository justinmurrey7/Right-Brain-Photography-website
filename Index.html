Every Moment Has a Story

Right Brain Photography began with a simple goal — to never let life’s most meaningful moments slip away. Founded by Justin Murrey after leaving a dead-end job, the camera became a way to slow time, capture emotion, and preserve memories for families, athletes, and communities. From rodeos and barrel racing to wildlife and local events, every image is created with heart, instinct, and authenticity.

What You Can’t Expect

And why that’s a good thing

Stiff Poses

You won’t be locked into awkward positions or forced smiles. If it feels unnatural, it won’t make the final gallery.

Rushed Sessions

Great images take patience. I don’t rush moments or treat your session like a time slot on a schedule.

Cookie-Cutter Editing

Presets don’t tell stories. Each image is edited individually to match the emotion and setting of the moment.

Disconnection

I’m not just showing up to take photos — I’m present, engaged, and invested in telling your story right.
